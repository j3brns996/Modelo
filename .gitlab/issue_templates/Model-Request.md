# Request a model review

An agent identifies the vendor and model, proposes an offering, and flags missing
facts for maintainer review. This request is not approval. Do not include secrets
or private commercial terms.

**Vendor, model, or link:** Name the vendor or model, or add an official link. Leave unknown details blank.

**Business need:** Who needs this, what business outcome matters, and why?

**Intended use:** What task will the model perform, with what data, and how will people check or act on the output?

**Where and when:** Service or provider, region, environment, and target date, if known. These help define the proposed offering.

**Model rights owner domicile:** Tick known jurisdictions for the legal rights owner, not the processing location. Add other countries in the vendor/model field. Leave unknown choices unticked.

**Operator or service provider:** Tick requested or known operators. Name another provider in Where and when. Leave unknown choices unticked.

**Data processing territory:** Tick requested or known processing locations. These are separate from the rights owner domicile. Add other locations in Where and when. Leave unknown choices unticked.
