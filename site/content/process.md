Modelo answers two separate questions: what does a provider say is available, and what has the organisation decided people may use?

A provider observation can supply facts about a model. It cannot grant enterprise approval. Approval exists only when the catalogue contains a current offering with a clear rationale, callable route and any conditions that apply.

Every change starts with an issue that explains the intended outcome and why it is needed. A pull request carries the exact proposed records. CI validates that exact Git revision, and a reviewer decides whether it should merge.

Published evidence is content-addressed. Once merged, it is not edited behind the same identifier; a refresh creates new evidence and moves the relevant fact links through review.
