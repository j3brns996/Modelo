How to read the catalogue

Available from a provider does not mean approved for your organisation.

A model record describes what exists. An offering records the enterprise decision: why it is approved, which service and route to use, and which conditions apply.

  1. 01Find the facts

    Read the provider source and record when and where the facts were seen.

  2. 02Keep the proof

    Link each published claim to a fixed, content-addressed evidence record.

  3. 03Make a decision

    Review the proposed change and explain why the offering should be approved.

  4. 04Publish exactly

    Build the reviewed Git revision once and publish those exact files.
